# Synthetic contract fixture — not market evidence

## Tension

**Observed:** A placeholder actor repeats a manual handoff. [Synthetic source](https://fixture-corpus-x.test-source.co/source)

**Inferred:** The scarce complement may be trusted coordination rather than the headline technology.

**Bet:** A narrow buyer will pay for that coordination. Wrong if the handoff has no meaningful cost.

## Venture

The venture mechanism begins service-first. The wedge is one recurring handoff for five consenting
pilot customers. The first-10 distribution path is a named professional community, not generic ads.
The counter-case is that incumbents can absorb the workflow. Use affordable loss: run the cheapest
manual test before software. If it works, process data and switching cost may compound into power.

## What they do today, and at what price

Before any product exists, this buyer already gets a version of this outcome `[needs current evidence]`: an internal
spreadsheet maintained by one person, an assistant who does it by hand between other duties, and a
free annual PDF that a consultancy publishes as a lead magnet. The spreadsheet costs roughly half a
day a week of someone already on payroll; the PDF costs nothing and is good enough for orientation.

[Inferred] That free-and-tolerable baseline, not any funded company, is the real competitor, and it
sets the price this venture must clear. What it structurally cannot produce is a dated, per-record
version the buyer can put in front of a third party at the moment a decision is made — which is the
only thing here worth paying for.

## Who already does this, and who they fail

An established operator sells the closest existing product to large enterprise accounts on annual
contracts with a professional-services onboarding `[needs current evidence]` — contract minimums were

[Inferred] They structurally cannot serve this segment: their cost floor is salaried delivery staff,

model cannot follow.
