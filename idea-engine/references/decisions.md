# Validation, Killer Prototype and Product Definition

## Validation after divergence
Attack the shortlist rather than competitor-filtering every initial seed. For each candidate record direct competitors and substitutes (including manual work), concrete why-now shifts, beneficiary/payer and willingness-to-pay proxies, differentiated behavior, biggest unknown, source dates/limits and the decision changed by evidence. Apply workflow.md evidence rules. A user's score or star is neither market evidence nor a payment commitment.

Use BUILD (ready for a bounded functional test), PROBE (resolve a crucial unknown), WATCH (specific enabling condition missing) or KILL (thesis invalidated), with rationale and next trigger. BUILD does not mean validated business or permission to execute. A direct equivalent can invalidate the proposed differentiation even for a 9★ favorite. Preserve that kill and its evidence; revive only with a materially changed thesis and new evidence, not a renamed incumbent. Competitor presence alone is not a universal kill rule.

## Killer Prototype contract
“Build the cheapest real thing that answers the biggest unknown.” Compare functional trial and proxy on actual cost, time, access and ability to answer that unknown. If AI makes a functional prototype comparably cheap and more informative, prefer it over a survey or landing page. For non-software businesses, a real delivered service or physical trial can be the prototype. A proxy remains appropriate when it answers the unknown better or a real trial is inaccessible; explain why. Do not inflate the scope to a full product.

Required plan fields:
- Biggest unknown and falsifiable thesis.
- Smallest real behavior, participant/first user and bounded time/cost; dependencies and what remains outside this test.
- Killer behavior and metric, observation window and eligible opportunities/denominator.
- Predefined PASS, INTERESTING and FAIL criteria, with a decision for each. Mark new thresholds as proposals, not historical user decisions. Make the branches mutually exclusive and exhaustive for observable outcomes, including partial success below the pass threshold. Classify insufficient eligible opportunities separately as inconclusive. Check one boundary example per branch before finalizing; no observed result should fall through the rubric.
- What dogfood can establish, what it cannot, and the next external-user/payment evidence needed.

The user can be user #0. Personal usefulness is valuable evidence, but does not establish a market, broad demand or willingness to pay. Initial delivery success does not answer repeat-use uncertainty; choose an observation window that can actually expose it.

## Stop condition and Product Definition
When selection and an informative bounded test exist, transition from generation to Product Definition; do not wait for perfect certainty or repeatedly ask for another ideation batch. Product Definition can precede prototype execution. If no candidate survives, record why and choose targeted exploration or close the campaign. Reopen divergence when evidence defeats the thesis or the user requests it, preserving the prior decision.

Product Definition contains:
1. Concrete problem and existing workaround.
2. Product primitive: the basic object or interaction that creates value.
3. First user, including who besides user #0 might use it.
4. Exactly 3–5 MVP capabilities tied to the unknown.
5. Explicit non-features that bound scope.
6. Behavioral differentiation from substitutes; evidence and remaining uncertainty.
7. Killer test, metric, observation window and pass/interesting/fail decisions.

Closeout records selected candidate/rationale, deferred tracks and re-entry triggers, killed versions/evidence, unresolved assumptions, prototype/definition, next action and outstanding feedback. Separate campaign-specific lessons from reusable process corrections. Apply the memory/version/eval loop before claiming an engine improvement. A closed campaign is resumable history; it does not authorize building, monitoring or external actions. Never default a new campaign to the previous winner.
